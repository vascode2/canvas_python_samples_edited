# Why canvas_python_samples_edited

## What it is

A modified copy of Ezurio's official **Canvas Python Samples**. I added **SNTP**, **HTTP** and **MQTT client** examples with expanded functionality, and integrated the existing snippets into **ready-to-run demos**, so a developer can evaluate the hardware without assembling the pieces first.

## Why I made it

I don't remember the details any more; the starting point was Ezurio's Canvas sample application. I wanted to extend those samples with functions we actually needed — SNTP, HTTP, MQTT.

I have little knowledge of Python and little programming experience, so I asked AI to do the work. Most of the code is the AI's.

## What came of it

I was able to present it to some distributors and to potential customers. I was very happy about that.

## On timing

Against the company's vision, the timing was not ideal. Coding has been lifted by AI: not many people care which language is being used any more — they describe what they want at a high level and manage the result. Because the AI writes the code, it matters little that these are Python wrappers around the C the chip vendor provides.
